GUTTERBALL

Game for Losers

Where losing wins!

The Rules

  1. Lowest score wins
  2. 10 Frames
  3. Bumpers on both sides must be up
  4. In the event of a split, and you cleanly split the split, the player is allowed to deduct the amount of pins split from their score
  5. No custom balls. Must use bowling alley balls
  6. If first ball stuck, second bowl knocks both balls into the gutter, player wins and game is over

Gutterball Terms

  • 1 pin down = ACE
  • Ride the rails
  • 2 pins = Duce
  • 3 pins = Trio
  • 2 gutterballs = Golden Gutter
