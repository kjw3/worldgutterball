GUTTERBALL

Where the Lowest Score Wins!

A Fun Twist on Traditional Bowling

Quick Start

  • Play 10 frames like regular bowling
  • Keep bumpers UP on both sides
  • Lowest score wins! Aim for the gutter!

How to Play

  1. Goal: The lowest score wins! Try to knock down the fewest pins. A Gutterball (0 pins) is the best shot!
  2. Frames: Play 10 frames just like regular bowling.
  3. Bumpers: Keep bumpers UP on both sides. This makes it harder to get a Gutterball!
  4. Split Bonus: If you leave two or more pins split apart, roll between them on your next throw without hitting any pins. If you succeed, subtract those standing pins from your score!
  5. Equipment: Use only the regular bowling balls that the alley provides. No special or custom balls allowed.
  6. Double Gutter Win: If your first ball gets stuck on the lane, try to knock it into the gutter with your second ball. If BOTH balls end up in the gutter (no pins knocked down, all 10 pins standing), you WIN instantly!
