How to Play

  1. The lowest score wins! Try to knock down the fewest pins.
  2. Play 10 frames just like regular bowling.
  3. Keep bumpers UP on both sides.
  4. Split Bonus: If pins are split apart, roll between them without hitting any. Subtract those standing pins from your score!
  5. Use bowling alley balls only - no special balls allowed.
  6. Double Gutter Win: If your first ball gets stuck, knock it into the gutter with your second ball. If both balls go in the gutter, you WIN instantly!

Any Standard Bowling Lane

You can play Gutterball on any normal bowling lane. The rules are the same as regular bowling, except the lowest score wins.
