Special Rules

SPLIT BONUS

Leave 2+ pins split apart? Roll between them on your next throw without hitting any pins. Succeed and subtract those standing pins from your score!

DOUBLE GUTTER WIN

First ball stuck on the lane? Knock it into the gutter with your second ball. If BOTH balls end up in the gutter (no pins down), you WIN instantly!
