How to Play

  1. The lowest score wins – the opposite of regular bowling.
  2. Play 10 frames just like regular bowling.
  3. Keep the bumpers up on both sides.
  4. If you leave two or more pins split, on your next throw try to roll the ball between them without hitting any pins. If you succeed, subtract the standing pins from your score.
  5. Use only the regular bowling balls that the alley provides.
  6. If your first ball gets stuck on the lane, you may try to knock it in with your second ball. If both balls end up in the gutter (no pins knocked down, all 10 pins standing), you win instantly.

Any Standard Bowling Lane

You can play Gutterball on any normal bowling lane. The rules are the same as regular bowling, except the lowest score wins.
