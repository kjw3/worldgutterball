How to Play

  1. Lowest score wins - flip traditional bowling on its head!
  2. Play 10 frames just like regular bowling
  3. Bumpers must be up on both sides
  4. In the event of a split, cleanly splitting the split deducts those pins from your score
  5. No custom balls - use standard bowling alley balls only
  6. If first ball gets stuck, and second bowl knocks both into gutter, you win instantly!

Any Standard Bowling Lane

Gutterball works on any regulation bowling lane. Use the standard scoring system with our exciting rule modifications!
